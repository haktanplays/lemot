# Cairn Social Layer — Founder Ratification Record v0.1

> **Read this first.** This document ratifies the Social Layer's **boundaries** and its **future direction**. It does **not** authorize implementation, does **not** make every conceptual primitive part of the product, and does **not** modify the Product Brain, Content Bible, mastery model, privacy runtime, or release scope. **The Social Charter remains Draft** until a separate sign-off review and promotion task.
>
> **Decision date: 2026-07-25.** Decisions recorded: **Q1** (root direction) and **R1–R11** (boundaries). Eight resolved, three dormant by decision.

---

## 1. Scope of the ratification

**What is ratified:**
- The **direction**: a structured, lesson-connected, heavily-moderated community layer is preserved as an explicit *future* product direction.
- The **boundaries** that would govern it: evidence, identity/privacy, gamification, under-13 participation, rejected forms, and governance.
- The **routing**: which questions are resolved, which are dormant, and what would reactivate them.

**What is NOT ratified:**
- No feature, no schedule, no design, no primitive selection, no build.
- No change to any other Bible, to mastery/evidence implementation, to privacy runtime, or to release scope.
- No promotion of the Charter to Canonical.

**Governing principle over everything below (R11):** documentation is not implementation authority.

## 2. Founder decisions R1–R11 (decision date 2026-07-25)

### R1 — Community form → **CONFIRM**

The preserved future direction is: **structured · heavily moderated · lesson-connected · bounded · learning-contextual.** It is **not** an open forum, **not** an infinite feed, **not** unrestricted general posting.

Remains a `RATIFIED_DIRECTION`, **not a build commitment**. The rejection of the classic open-forum form remains **binding**. *(Register: SOC-030, SOC-031.)*

### R2 — Lifetime loyalty community home → **EXTERNAL FOR CURRENT SCOPE**

- The founder/Lifetime loyalty community remains **external** for the current product scope.
- **No in-app loyalty-community surface is approved.**
- Possible future in-app integration remains `DEFERRED` — **never label it `PLANNED`.**
- The **loyalty community and the learner-learning community are separate concepts.** Neither automatically inherits the other's permissions, moderation model, or product surface. *(Register: SOC-009, SOC-020.)*

### R3 — First community primitives → **LEAVE DORMANT**

Lesson-specific questions, approved explanations, highlighted answers, structured reflections, and bounded attempt comparison remain **conceptual primitives inside the ratified direction**. **No first primitive is selected for design or implementation.** *(Register: SOC-032.)*

### R4 — Peer practice / speaking partner → **LEAVE DORMANT**

Bounded AI remains sufficient for the current solo-first product. **No speaking-partner, peer-practice, matching, or language-exchange system is approved.** Preserved as `OPEN` and dormant. *(Register: SOC-018.)*

### R5 — Profile and identity privacy default → **RATIFY PRIVACY-SAFE DEFAULT**

**Binding boundary — by default:**
- no public real-name profile;
- no public discoverable learner profile;
- no public social graph;
- **no profile is required for the current solo-first experience.**

**If a future structured community layer is explicitly opened:**
- minimum viable identity must be **pseudonymous or otherwise privacy-preserving**;
- visibility must be **opt-in**;
- identity fields must be **minimized**;
- profile discoverability must **not be silently enabled**;
- **real-name exposure requires a separate explicit decision and privacy justification.**

**This decision does not approve profiles as a feature.** It defines the default boundary *if* a future Social surface requires identity. Recorded as a founder-ratified privacy boundary. *(Register: **SOC-033** (new), bounding SOC-019.)*

### R6 — Broad sharing and UGC → **REJECT BROAD PUBLIC UGC / COMMUNITY FEED**

**Rejected forms:** open public posting · unrestricted learner-generated-content feeds · public mistake feeds · public performance feeds · infinite community content · popularity-driven content distribution · weakly-moderated public sharing.

> ⚠️ **Do not interpret this as rejecting every bounded community contribution.**

The following remain **possible only inside the ratified structured-community direction and require separate approval** — they are neither approved nor rejected by R6: lesson-specific questions · bounded attempt comparison · approved explanations · moderated reflections · selected or reviewed contributions.

**Separate question, kept `OPEN`:** user-initiated **outward** sharing (a learner deliberately sharing a milestone outside Cairn) is a Product/UX/Brand/Privacy question — **not** an in-app social feed and must never be treated as one. *(Register: SOC-021 `REJECTED`; SOC-024 `OPEN`, re-routed.)*

### R7 — Cohort or group learning → **LEAVE DORMANT**

**No learner cohort, classroom, group-learning, or community-group system is approved.** Operational tester cohorts remain **non-social** and **must not be used as evidence for learner-community design.** *(Register: SOC-023; cf. SOC-015.)*

### R8 — Social and mastery evidence → **RATIFY EVIDENCE FIREWALL WITH PRECISE BOUNDARY**

**Superseded (do not use):** *"No activity occurring in a social context can ever become mastery evidence."*

**Binding rule:**

> **Social engagement signals do not constitute mastery evidence. A discrete pedagogical action performed within a future social context may count as learning evidence only if it is governed by a separately ratified evidence contract and satisfies the same validity, prerequisite, attribution, anti-gaming, and error-source requirements as an equivalent non-social action.**

**Never constitute mastery evidence by themselves:** likes · reactions · replies · posting frequency · time spent in community · participation count · popularity · reputation · partner presence · receiving or giving a correction · social completion · community streaks · peer approval · teacher or moderator approval without an evidence contract.

**A future speaking or writing action may become evidence only when:**
- the action itself is pedagogically defined;
- learner authorship is attributable;
- assistance level is known;
- prerequisites are safe;
- the result is evaluated by an approved evidence contract;
- content, validator, peer, AI, or UI errors are excluded from learner weakness;
- the action would satisfy equivalent standards outside Social.

**Social context alone must never upgrade an action into evidence.**

**Current state:** **no evidence contract exists**, therefore **no social action is evidence today.** This decision does **not** alter the Content Bible or the current mastery implementation. *(Register: SOC-026.)*

### R9 — Anti-gamification firewall → **YES — PERMANENT SOCIAL-LAYER PRINCIPLE**

Future Social features must not introduce: friend streaks · social XP · public ranks · leaderboards · popularity levels · competitive progress comparison · "someone passed you" messaging · pressure-based participation · engagement rewards disconnected from learning value.

**Narrow carve-out:** trust-and-safety signals may exist **operationally**, but **must not be presented as popularity, learner worth, or learning mastery.** *(Register: **SOC-034** (new); cf. SOC-002/003/004.)*

### R10 — Under-13 participation → **LOCKED CURRENT SCOPE, WITH EXPLICIT REOPEN GATE**

**Superseded (do not use):** *"Permanently prohibited regardless of any future audience or product change."*

**Binding rule:**

> **Cairn does not permit under-13 participation in learner-to-learner Social systems under the current product scope. This boundary may be reopened only through a new explicit founder and canon decision after child-safety, parental-consent, legal, privacy, moderation, data-retention, identity, and operational requirements have been fully designed and independently reviewed.**

**Until all such requirements and the explicit reopening decision exist:**
- no under-13 Social pilot;
- no child account interaction;
- no adult–minor matching;
- no direct communication involving under-13 users;
- **no assumption that general age-policy changes automatically open Social access.**

This is a **locked current-scope safety boundary with a high-threshold reopening protocol** — not a silent permanent claim about every possible future Cairn product. *(Register: SOC-028.)*

### R11 — Governance protocol → **ACCEPT**

> **Nothing may move from `OPEN`, `DEFERRED`, `EXPERIMENT`, or `RATIFIED_DIRECTION` into design or implementation merely because an agent finds it documented.**

Social work may begin **only** when the founder explicitly opens a scoped Social task. **The opening decision must state all ten:**

1. exact primitive or feature
2. target user
3. intended value
4. current status
5. safety prerequisites
6. affected Bibles
7. evidence relationship
8. implementation boundary
9. validation plan
10. stop condition

**A general instruction such as "continue the project" or "build the future systems" is NOT sufficient authority to begin Social implementation.** *(Register: SOC-025.)*

## 5. Ratified invariants

1. **Evidence firewall (R8 / SOC-026)** — engagement is never evidence; a pedagogical action may be, only under a separately ratified evidence contract meeting equivalent non-social standards. No contract exists → nothing social is evidence today.
2. **Anti-gamification, permanent (R9 / SOC-034)** — no social streaks, XP, ranks, leaderboards, popularity, competitive comparison, or engagement rewards; trust/safety signals operational only.
3. **Under-13 locked for current scope, high-threshold reopen gate (R10 / SOC-028).**
4. **Privacy-safe identity default (R5 / SOC-033)** — no public/real-name/discoverable profile or social graph by default; pseudonymous, opt-in, minimized if ever opened.
5. **Governance: documentation is not implementation authority (R11 / SOC-025)** — ten-element scoped founder opening required.
6. **Inherited and unchanged:** privacy local-first + consent-gated (ADR-0023 / SOC-006), passive-mirror dignity (ADR-0002 / SOC-005), no pressure language (SOC-004).

## 6. Ratified future direction

A **structured · heavily moderated · lesson-connected · bounded · learning-contextual** community layer, whose learner value is to **ask questions, compare attempts, access alternative explanations, feel less alone, and develop belonging around learning French.**

**Conceptual primitives inside the direction** (possibilities, **not** separately approved features): lesson-specific questions · approved explanations · teacher-highlighted answers · authority-bounded AI-highlighted answers · structured community reflections · bounded comparison of learner attempts.

**Status:** `RATIFIED_DIRECTION` (SOC-029/030). **Implementation state:** not implemented · not planned for the current build · deferred/post-MVP · **requires future founder and cross-layer decisions.**

## 7. Explicitly rejected forms

- **Classic open / chaotic forum** (R1/SOC-031): Reddit-style open posting, infinite feed, unrestricted free-form conversation, weakly-moderated general discussion disconnected from lessons.
- **Broad public UGC / community feed** (R6/SOC-021): open public posting, unrestricted learner-content feeds, public mistake feeds, public performance feeds, infinite community content, popularity-driven distribution, weakly-moderated public sharing.
- **Social gamification** (R9/SOC-034): friend streaks, social XP, public ranks, leaderboards, popularity levels, competitive comparison, "someone passed you" messaging.

**These reject *forms*, not community itself.** Bounded, moderated contributions inside the ratified direction remain possible and require separate approval.

## 8. Non-claims

This record does **NOT**:

- authorize any Social implementation, design, prototype, or schedule;
- make every conceptual primitive part of the product;
- approve profiles, follows, direct messaging, partner matching, social graphs, peer grading, public progress, public mistakes, live audio/video, unrestricted comments, teacher marketplaces, mentor systems, referral mechanics, or cohort systems — **all remain unapproved**;
- modify the Product Brain, Content Bible, Curriculum, Brand, UX, or Engineering bibles;
- modify mastery, evidence, progression, or privacy **runtime** behaviour;
- change release scope or the current solo-first product;
- promote the Social Charter to Canonical;
- assert that Cairn is permanently solo, or that Social is a core product pillar.

**Silence is never approval.** Anything not explicitly decided here remains unapproved.

## 10. Reopen protocol

**To begin any Social work (R11):** the founder issues an explicit, scoped opening stating all ten elements (§2/R11). Then the gate sequence in Charter §19 runs: §5 invariant pass/fail → Privacy-Legal data-sharing + consent surface → passive-mirror / evidence-firewall / anti-gamification / identity-default reviews → `EXPERIMENT` with success criteria → Content-Bible stage-gate discipline before any learner exposure.

**To reactivate a dormant question (R3/R4/R7):** an explicit founder opening, or discovery of stronger historical source evidence.

**To reopen under-13 (R10):** a new explicit founder **and canon** decision, only after child-safety, parental-consent, legal, privacy, moderation, data-retention, identity, and operational requirements are **fully designed and independently reviewed**. A general age-policy change is explicitly **insufficient**.

**To allow a social action to count as evidence (R8):** a **separately ratified evidence contract** meeting all seven conditions in §2/R8.
