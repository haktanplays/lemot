> ## Ratified L17 scope (2026-08-08) — read before anything below
>
> A founder audit against **shipped** L0–L16 content settled the open questions and corrected a load-bearing error in this spec. Where older text below disagrees, this banner wins.
>
> **1 · The "wide owned state band" premise was false.** This spec and its gate review justified their scope on the claim that the learner *"can already express a wide band of human states — tired, hungry, thirsty, afraid, not ready, needing help, not understanding, it's okay."* Of that list exactly **one** item ships: `j'ai faim` (L4). **`fatigué`, `soif`, `peur`, `besoin d'aide`, first-person `prêt`, `étudiant`, `mais`, `c'est pas grave`, `je ne comprends pas` and `vous pouvez répéter` are NOT owned** — the first has no identity at all, and the repair pair exists only as PR-07 L1-pilot registrations that no shipped lesson works. None is provisioned here; each is separate curriculum work. **L17's scope must not depend on any of them.**
>
> **2 · Journey role: `standard`.** Chosen pedagogically — **L17 extends expressive/social range inside already-owned frames and opens no new grammatical engine or architecture.** Every shipped Doorway (L7–L9, L11, L12, L14, L15) opens an engine; L17 explicitly opens none, and `ça va` carries a guard against opening `aller`/futur. *(Budget result, recorded separately so it is not mistaken for the reason: Standard band 1–4, L17 count 3, **PASS**.)*
>
> **3 · Active-new is 3, not 5 and not "~3–5".** Exact demands:
> ```
> chunk-ca-va · adj-fatigue · adj-content
> ```
> This also lands inside PRJ-015 IC-002's *normal target* of 1–3, so no fourth-demand cognitive-load rationale is required.
>
> **4 · `Ça va ?` and `Ça va.` are ONE identity: `chunk-ca-va`.** The rising-intonation ask is a **use** of the frozen chunk, exactly as `Je peux faire une pause ?` is a use of `chunk-je-peux` — punctuation does not mint an identity. **`chunk-ca-va-question` is not created**, and no `acquisitionLink` is used to split or cluster the pair.
>
> **5 · `Ça ne va pas.` is composed, not an identity.** It is `chunk-ca-va` plus the already-owned `ne…pas` operation (L3), following the shipped `je ne peux pas` canon (`lesson-013.ts`: composed as model copy, *"never as a chip"*). **`chunk-ca-ne-va-pas` is not created** — not as a demand, not as supported, not as a chip, not as a link target. *(Known authoring risk, left visible rather than solved by minting an id: `va` stays opaque inside the frozen formula, so the composition is less transparent than `je ne peux pas` was.)*
>
> **6 · Two state adjectives, one identity each.** `adj-fatigue` (`fatigué` / `fatiguée`) and `adj-content` (`content` / `contente`), both composing with the owned `chunk-je-suis`. **Feminine forms are authored surfaces, never separate ids** (PRJ-015 IC-005 names `content`/`contente` as its own worked example). No `chunk-je-suis-fatigue` composite, no `word-fatigue`, no `acquisitionLink`/`acquisitionComponents`. **`fatigué` is NEW in L17** — the L02 spec planned it and the shipped L2 compact slice dropped it; it was never actually recycled.
>
> **7 · The `je comprends` promotion is de-scoped.** You cannot promote the affirmative of a chunk the learner never acquired: its source, `je ne comprends pas`, is part of the orphaned repair rail. **`chunk-je-comprends` is not created** and L17 declares no demand for it.

> **Archetype weighting (read first).** Primary = **Thematic Vocabulary / Context (#9)** run with **Review/Integration (#10) restraint** (recycle-dominant; feelings in owned frames; Opening Sentence Family / Mon Lexique / Say It Your Way strong; new-grammar light). Secondary flavor = **Chunk / Natural Speech (#1)** (`ça va ?` as the near-chunk vehicle). Not a second budget.

## 2. Owned Target

**Social check-in chunks (primary) + one capped positive adjective + recycled states (no new system):**

- **Social check-in (new — `chunk-ca-va`):** one frozen formula with two uses — `Ça va ?` asked with a rising voice, `Ça va.` answered with a falling one. **Not** productive `aller`, **not** futur proche. The negative answer `Ça ne va pas.` is **composed** from this chunk plus the owned `ne…pas` operation (L3) — it is not a second identity.
- **Positive state (new — `adj-content`):** `Je suis content.` (m) / `Je suis contente.` (f) in the **owned `je suis + state` frame** — the learner's first *positive* feeling. Fixed pair, **not** a taught agreement rule.
- **Negative state (new — `adj-fatigue`):** `Je suis fatigué.` / `Je suis fatiguée.` in the same frame. **NEW in L17, not recycled** — the L02 spec planned `adj-fatigue` and the shipped L2 compact slice dropped it, so no learner has ever owned it. It is what makes `Ça ne va pas.` answerable with a reason.
- **Recycled / recombined (verified against shipped L0–L16 only):** `j'ai faim` (L4) · `je ne suis pas` + the `ne…pas` sandwich (L3) · `il faut faire une pause` / `je dois + inf.` (L15/L9, obligation — never advice) · `est-ce que` (L12) · `je peux` (L11) · `j'y vais` / `on y va` (L14, only if the place is already clear) · `bonjour` / `merci` / `au revoir` (L0/L1/L6).
- ~~**Light promotion:** `je comprends`~~ — **DE-SCOPED**: its source `je ne comprends pas` is part of the orphaned repair rail and was never worked in shipped content. See banner §7.

**Clarify:**
- L17 owns **simple human-state response**, **not emotional depth**.
- The learner should feel: **"I can check in and respond humanly — without giving advice."**
- The genuinely new lexis is **three items**: `chunk-ca-va` + `adj-content` + `adj-fatigue`. Everything else is recombination.

## 3. Not Owned / Deferred (explicit)

Recognition-only or deferred — never production targets in L17:
- **broad emotion vocabulary** — `triste`/`anxieux`/`déprimé`/`seul`/`énervé`/`déçu` as an active set; **`triste` is recognition only**; no word-list.
- **`se sentir` / `je me sens …`** — reflexive-verb system; **recognition / future-hook only**, never produced.
- **advice / conditionnel** (`tu devrais` / `il faudrait` / `je devrais`) — the band map's "light advice", **deferred to paid-zone** (L15).
- **therapy / coaching / moralizing tone** (incl. moralizing via `il faut`/`je dois`).
- **emotional personalization** (tailoring feelings content to the learner).
- **past/future feeling narrative** (`hier j'étais triste`, `demain ça ira`, `ça va aller`) — feelings stay **present**.
- **futur proche** (`je vais + inf.`) — **recognition / future-hook for L18 only**, never owned here.
- **`je vais bien`** (the `aller`-state answer to `ça va ?`) — **recognition/deferred** (reuses `aller` in a non-movement sense + adds `bien`); owned answers are `ça va` / `je suis content(e)`.
- **adjective gender-agreement system** — `content`/`contente` shown as a fixed pair; the agreement *rule* is not taught.
- **object-pronoun production** (`je peux vous aider` / `aidez-moi`) — `m'aider` stays frozen inside `vous pouvez m'aider`.
- **full / deep A Small Moment emotional recurrence** + **paid-depth AI emotional/social feedback** (paid-zone).

## 5. Candidate Scene / Sentence Family

> Scene (small human moment, after class / before leaving): *"You check in with someone. They're not okay — they're tired. A break is needed; then you head off."* The check-in (`Ça va ?` → feeling answer) is the spine. *(The briefed "don't understand / you reassure them" beats are removed: both depend on the orphaned repair rail and `c'est pas grave`.)*

| Role | Sentence | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Check-in (anchor, new)** | `Ça va ?` | **new core**: social check-in, frozen chunk |
| **Negative answer (composed + new adjective)** | `Ça ne va pas. Je suis fatigué(e).` | `ça va` + the owned `ne…pas` (L3) — composed, not an id — then **new** `adj-fatigue` |

| ~~Reassure~~ | ~~`C'est pas grave.`~~ | **REMOVED — no canonical identity exists** |
| **Obligation, NOT advice (recycled)** | `Il faut faire une pause.` | L15 — "one must," **not** "you should" |
| **Leave (recycled, optional)** | `J'y vais.` | L14 place-`y` — only if the place is already clear |

- **Anchor:** `Ça va ?` · **Variations:** `Ça va.`, `Ça ne va pas.`, `Je suis content(e).`, `Je suis fatigué(e).` · **Contrasts:** `Ça va ?` (frozen social) vs `Je vais à la maison.` (movement `aller`, L7 — *different `aller`*); `Il faut faire une pause.` (obligation) vs `Tu devrais faire une pause.` (**advice — forbidden**).

## 8. Exercise Flow — Compact (thematic + integration restraint)

> Weighting: **Opening Sentence Family / Weave It / Say It Your Way / Natural Reveal carry the lesson; Why This Works light.** **Focus: human check-in + simple state response — no advice, no emotional coaching, no broad emotion list.**

| Section | Purpose | Learner action | Feedback |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Meet It** | Meet the check-in moment | listen `Ça va ? — Ça ne va pas. Je suis fatigué(e).` (TTS, FR only) | passive mirror |
| **Notice the Pieces** | See `ça va` (frozen) + `je suis + feeling`; note `ça va` ≠ movement `aller` | tap `ça va` / `je suis` / the feeling | neutral |
| **Try It** | Pick the right feeling/answer; reject traps | choose `Je suis content(e)` / `Ça ne va pas`; reject `Tu devrais…`, `Je me sens…`, `Ça va aller`, `Je vais bien`, an emotion word-list | reveal + reason |
| **Weave It** | Build a check-in + feeling line | assemble `Ça va ? — Non, je suis fatigué(e).` / `Ça va. Je suis content(e).` | model + alternatives |
| **Say It Your Way** | Answer "ça va?" / say how you are | "Someone asks how you are — say if you're okay or not, and how you feel." | model-answer-only (no AI) + natural alternatives |
| **Optional tiny check-in moment (A Small Moment, light)** | Read a ≤2–3 line feelings moment, respond once | comprehension check or one short scaffolded response — present-only, known-items-only | **deterministic / model-answer reveal** — no AI, no chat (references L16; does **not** expand the feature) |

| **Lesson End** | Calm close, name the capability | read recap | passive mirror ("You can ask how someone is — and say how you are.") |

## 9. Natural Reveal / Feedback

**Check-in / feeling (signature):**
- **Expected:** `Ça va ?` / `Ça va.` / `Ça ne va pas. Je suis fatigué(e).` / `Je suis content(e).`
- **Acceptable alternatives:** `Non, ça ne va pas`; `Je suis fatigué(e).` / `Je suis content(e).` as answers; `J'ai faim.` where the scene invites it. *(`j'ai besoin d'aide` struck — not owned. `ça va bien` struck — `bien` is not owned either.)*

**Common mistakes (watch list):**
- **over-answering with new emotions** (importing `anxieux`, `déprimé`, `seul`…) — hint: "say it with what you know — `je suis content(e)` / `je suis fatigué`." (`trap:emotion-vocab-dump`)
- **using `se sentir` too early** (`Je me sens fatigué.`) — hint: "for now, `je suis fatigué`." (`trap:se-sentir-too-early`)
- **giving advice with `tu devrais`** (`Tu devrais faire une pause.`) — hint: "say how you feel, or what *one must* do (`il faut faire une pause`) — not advice." (`trap:conditionnel-advice-too-early`)
- **using past/future** (`Hier, j'étais triste.` / `Demain, ça ira.`) — hint: "stay in the present — `ça va` / `ça ne va pas`." (`trap:past-future-feeling-narrative`)
- **turning `ça va` into `aller`/futur logic** (`Ça va aller.` / `Je vais bien` as a taught form) — hint: "`ça va` is a set phrase; keep it as is." (`trap:ca-va-aller-future-leak`)
- **object pronoun** (`Je peux vous aider.`) — hint: "keep `m'aider` inside `vous pouvez m'aider`." *(The former workaround `je peux faire ça` is withdrawn — `faire ça` is not owned either.)* (`trap:object-pronoun-pressure-too-early`)
- **therapy/coach tone / writing too long** — one short, kind line; passive mirror only; no coaching.

> Feedback style: **passive mirror**; "our expected target was…"; **natural alternative if within scope**; recognition forms (`je me sens`, `tu devrais`, `ça va aller`, `je vais bien`) are **redirected, never taught on the spot** (contract §11). **No score / reward / streak language; no therapy/coach tone.**

## 12. QA / Pilot Findings

- **Is a compact spec enough?** **Yes.** 0 new systems, 0 new architecture verbs, 3 new lexical items over owned frames; mostly recombination. A full spec would invite the emotion-vocabulary tables the gate forbids.
- **Is L17 too light after L16?** **No — appropriately light, and differentiated.** L16 was integration + reading seed; L17 adds a genuinely *new human capability* (the social check-in `ça va ?`) plus the first *positive* feeling — neither of which L16 covered. It is recycle-dominant but not flat: the learner can now open and answer "how are you?", which the free tier could not do before.
- **Does `ça va` risk confusing `aller`/futur?** **A real but contained risk.** `ça va` literally is `aller` 3rd-person, and `ça va aller` (futur proche) is the natural drift. Contained by: owning `ça va` as a **frozen chunk** (homograph guard, distinct from `word:aller`), blocking `ça va aller` / `je vais bien` (`trap:ca-va-aller-future-leak`), and keeping `aller` movement-only (L7). Worth watching in smoke; if confusion appears, lean harder on "set phrase, learn it whole."
- **Does `content`/`contente` add enough novelty without becoming a vocab dump?** **Yes — it is the right amount.** One positive adjective gives the learner their first *positive* self-state — high emotional usefulness, near-zero load. **Corrected:** the old claim that "all prior states are negative/neutral" overstated the inventory. Before L17 the learner ships **one** state, `j'ai faim`; `fatigué` is why L17 also owns the negative half. The gendered pair is shown as a fixed alternant, not an agreement rule. More adjectives would tip into a dump; `triste` stays recognition-only.
- **Is advice fully blocked?** **Yes** — conditionnel/advice deferred (paid-zone); `il faut`/`je dois` used as obligation, not coaching; `trap:conditionnel-advice-too-early`; passive mirror throughout.
- **Is `se sentir` fully blocked?** **Yes** — recognition / future-hook only, 0 production; `trap:se-sentir-too-early`.
- **Should L18 remain Futur-proche stronger preview?** **Yes** — L17's `je vais + inf.` recognition hook sets it up; L17 does **not** own futur proche.
- **Is the journey role right?** **Yes — `standard`, on pedagogical grounds.** L17 extends expressive/social range inside already-owned frames and opens no new grammatical engine; every shipped Doorway opens one. The budget result is recorded separately and did not drive the choice: Standard band 1–4, count 3, **PASS**.
- **Why is `fatigué` new rather than recycled?** Because no learner has ever owned it. The L02 spec planned `adj-fatigue` as active-new; the shipped L2 compact slice dropped it, and L10/L13 both de-scoped lines that assumed it. L17 is its first real home — a feelings lesson using the `je suis + state` frame is exactly where it belongs.
- **Top risks:** (1) **emotion-vocabulary dump** (the #1 leak — capped at two adjectives); (2) **advice/therapy tone** (deferred); (3) **`ça va` → `aller`/futur confusion** (frozen-chunk guard + trap); (4) **`se sentir`** (recognition only); (5) **past/future feeling narrative + object-pronoun pressure**; (6) **phantom ownership** — the failure that this reconciliation corrected, and the reason the recycled column is now verified against shipped content rather than against planning prose. All guarded by §3/§6/§9/§10 + six traps.
