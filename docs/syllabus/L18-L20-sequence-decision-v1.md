## 2. What is retired: futur proche as L18's job — **SUPERSEDED**

The band map (§3 L10–L20 table, §4 Option C, §5, §7, §10 gates 4/11, §12) proposes **L18 = "Futur proche stronger preview (NOT owned)"** — a lesson whose entire job is a recognition-only preview of `je vais + inf.`, with "~3–5 (mostly recog)" and "0 active ownership."

**That proposal is retired as L18's primary job.** It is marked **SUPERSEDED**, not deleted — the analysis behind it (that futur proche is the single strongest free-tier temptation and must stay reserved pre-Campfire) is **retained and still governs**; only its *packaging as a standalone lesson* is retired.

**Why it is retired (founder decision, 2026-08-08):**

1. **A zero-demand preview lesson has no legal journey role.** Doorway requires 1–2 active-new and Standard requires 1–4 (Content Bible §6.8.1); a lesson whose declared purpose is *"0 active ownership"* cannot satisfy either. Integration, Review, and Milestone all describe a consolidation or proof job that a forward preview is not doing. **No sixth journey role may be added** (§6.8.1, explicit: *"There is no `Hybrid` journey role, and none may be added"*). A standalone futur-preview lesson is therefore structurally unbuildable under ratified canon.
2. **Forward-seeding does not need its own lesson.** Content Bible §6.8.4 already licenses recognition-only forward seeding *inside* any lesson without it counting as active-new, and names L10→L11 and L13→L14 as worked precedents. A preview is a **beat**, not a lesson.
3. **The band already tried this and narrowed it.** L16's gate review §11 gate 5 required exactly one recognition-only forward hook; the founder **narrowed that to permitted-but-not-required**, and shipped L16 carries **none**. The same reasoning applies here.

**What survives unchanged:** futur proche remains **reserved pre-Campfire**. No lesson in L18–L20 owns it, previews it as a production target, or graduates it to supported. The Charter's open item — *"futur-proche ownership (L18 preview-only is provisional; no pre-L24 production ownership may be inferred)"* (§13) — stays open, and this decision **narrows** it rather than resolving it: L18 preview-only is now retired as a plan, and no replacement pre-L24 ownership is created.

## 3. FP-C — where the futur hook actually goes

**Ratified: FP-C — one bounded recognition-only futur-proche hook at the END of L20.**

| Property | Ruling |
|---|---|
| Placement | the **closing beat of L20**, after the capability proof, facing Campfire |
| Acquisition demands | **0** |
| Canonical identities created | **0** |
| Production target | **none** — never produced, never scored, never a model answer the learner must reach |
| Status | **recognition-only** |
| What it may claim | that something is coming |
| What it may **not** claim | that L7 or L17 previewed it (see §5) |

**Why the end of L20 and not earlier.** L20's job is a capability proof facing Campfire; a "here is what is next" beat is native to a Milestone/Transition lesson (archetype #11) and foreign to an Integration one. Placing it at the end also keeps it structurally incapable of contaminating the proof — the learner has already demonstrated everything before they meet it.

**Why it is safe.** With 0 demands and 0 identities it consumes no journey-role budget and creates no registry obligation. It is a **beat inside a ratified lesson**, which is exactly the shape §2 says a preview should have.

## 4. Lesson decisions

### 4.1 L18 — Question Expansion 2 (`comment`)

Settled in full by **`docs/syllabus/L18-question-expansion-2-gate-review.md`**. Sequence-level summary:

- **Job:** *controlled Question Expansion 2 — introduce one productive question-word slot without opening the full French question system.*
- **Journey role: `doorway`**, chosen **pedagogically**: L18 opens one bounded productive question-word capability that did not exist before. The band result (1–2, count 1) is a **check**, not the reason.
- **Acquisition: `["adverb-comment"]`** — exactly one demand.
- **The Q-word is `comment` and only `comment`.** The band map's free-tier allowance reads *"L12 `est-ce que` + **1–2 Q-words**"* (§5) — L12 spent **zero** of it. **L18 owns exactly ONE.** The second permitted Q-word is **not** taken merely because the band permits it.
- **`Comment ?` as repair is explicitly blocked/deferred.** L18 is not the vehicle for solving the repair rail (§6, RR-A).
- **L18 is Question Expansion 2's home.** L12's compact spec §3 and its Carry-Out, and L13's compact spec §3 and Carry-Out, all route question words to *"a later Question Expansion 2"*. That lesson now has a number.

### 4.2 L19 — Integration + weak-point repair + light ASM recurrence

- **Job ratified as proposed** by the band map: recombine the band's engines, recover what is weak, and carry a **light second A Small Moment recurrence**.
- **Journey role: `integration`. Acquisition: `[]` (zero).** Plain band compliance — **no exception is requested or granted**, matching shipped L10, L13, and L16.
- **"Weak-point repair" means the mastery/review mechanism, NOT French conversational repair language.** See §6 — this is a required disambiguation, not a stylistic note.
- **The differentiator from L16 is the second ASM recurrence**, and it is bounded by exactly the constraints L16's seed established: **known-items-only · present-scope · model-answer / scaffold-constrained · 0 active-new · no live AI, no free-text evaluation, no personalization**. It recurs the form; it does not enlarge it. The deep AI-driven A Small Moment stays paid-zone (band map §5).
- L19 is the **second** integration beat in three lessons' distance from L16, so the "too little novelty" risk the archetype names applies here exactly as it did at L16. The ASM recurrence plus the repair function are what answer it — **not** a new grammar system and **not** a new lexical item.

### 4.3 L20 — Pre-Campfire capability checkpoint

- **Journey role: `milestone`. Acquisition: `[]` (zero).**
- **L20's job is proof, not teaching.** It introduces no capability. Everything it exercises must already be owned at L19's exit.
- **L20 would be the first shipped Milestone lesson.** Content Bible §6.8.5 records that *"Review and Milestone have no shipped examples, so their contracts remain empirically unvalidated."* L20 is therefore also the **first empirical test of the Milestone contract** — its authoring should be treated as contract validation work, and any friction it exposes in the 0–3 band or the Milestone/Transition archetype is a **finding to record**, not a defect to paper over.
- **Exit-proof concept (concept, not a script):**

  > **The learner carries one small French moment end to end, with minimal or no supplied pieces, recombining the capabilities they genuinely own through L19.**

  The moment should span: **greet → ask → answer → state a need → express obligation, permission, or movement → close.**

- **No exact script, scene, or line set is frozen here.** The compact spec chooses the realization. What is fixed is the *shape* of the proof and the constraint that every piece of it is already owned.
- **L20 tests integrated capability, not cumulative vocabulary recall.** It is not a vocabulary audit, not a coverage sweep of every item shipped since L1, and not a scored exam. A learner who can carry the moment passes even if individual items from earlier lessons never appear.
- **The FP-C hook closes the lesson** (§3), after the proof.

## 5. Corrections to prior canon

These are **documentation corrections**. They correct claims about shipped lessons that the shipped lessons do not support. **No lesson is reopened, no content is edited, and no identity is added or removed by any of them.**

### 5.1 The L7 futur-proche hook does not exist

**Claim in force:** band map §5 — *"futur proche | early-free PREVIEW only (**L7 hook** → L18 stronger preview)"*; also implied by §3's L18 row (*"Major recycled engines: aller, faire, infinitive"*).

**Evidence:** shipped `lemot-app/content/lessons/v1/lesson-007.ts` contains `je vais` **only** as `Je vais à la maison.` — the frozen movement chunk. Its own learner copy defines it as *"Je vais = I'm going. The moving engine."* There is **no `je vais + infinitive` anywhere in the lesson**, in any status. The AI contract §15 L7 row is consistent with the shipped lesson and already blocks the form: *"`je vais + infinitive` (futur proche) is recognition-only, **never produced**"* — but "recognition-only" there describes a **blocked** form, not a hook the lesson carries.

**Correction:** shipped L7 carries **no futur-proche hook**. It is a movement doorway on a frozen chunk. Any downstream reasoning that treats L7 as having pre-seeded futur proche is **withdrawn**. L7 itself is **not reopened** — the lesson is correct; only the claim about it was wrong.

### 5.2 The L17 futur-proche hook was never carried

**Claim in force:** L17's gate review §4 — *"L17 may carry **one recognition-only hook for L18** (`je vais + inf.`, recognition since L7)"*; band map §7's L17 mitigation row lists it in the recognition estimate.

**Evidence:** shipped `lemot-app/content/lessons/v1/lesson-017.ts` contains **zero** occurrences of `je vais`. The hook was permitted, never authored.

**Correction:** shipped L17 carries **no futur-proche hook**. The gate review's wording was permissive (*"may carry"*), so this is a stale expectation rather than a contradiction — but combined with §5.1 it means **no shipped lesson has previewed futur proche at all**, and FP-C (§3) must not be described as "landing" a preview that L7 or L17 began. It is the **first**.

### 5.3 The `faire` carry-in is phantom ownership

**Claim in force:** band map §3, L18 row — *"Major recycled engines: **aller, faire, infinitive**."*

**Evidence:** `lemot-app/content/itemRegistry.ts` contains `verb-etre`, `verb-aider`, and `verb-aller`, and **no `verb-faire`**. `faire` exists only inside the frozen package `chunk-faire-une-pause` (and its `chunk-je-voudrais-faire-une-pause` relative). The learner owns *"take a break"* as a unit; they do not own `faire`. Separately, **no infinitive slot is owned anywhere in the shipped corpus** — `verb-aller` is `status: "supported"` and reachable only after `il faut` / `je dois`, which are themselves frozen surfaces.

**Correction:** L18 may recycle **`chunk-faire-une-pause` as a package**. It may **not** treat `faire`, a `faire` paradigm, or a general infinitive slot as owned material. This is the same **phantom-ownership** failure class that produced the L10, L13, and L16 canonical blockers: a planning spec's inventory was inherited downstream as if it had shipped. It is recorded here so L18's compact spec does not repeat it a fourth time.

### 5.4 "Repair" is overloaded and must be disambiguated

Two unrelated things in this repo are called *repair*:

| Sense | Meaning | Where it appears | L19 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mastery repair** | the SRS / weak-point recovery **mechanism** — surfacing items the learner has got wrong and giving them another retrieval opportunity | band map §3 L19 row (*"weak-point repair pool"*, *"mastery events"*), §5 Practice Pool rows | **this is L19's** |
| **Conversational repair** | French **language** for recovering a broken exchange — `je ne comprends pas`, `vous pouvez répéter ?`, `c'est pas grave`, and `Comment ?` | the orphaned PR-07 L1-pilot registrations; the retired L16 repair beat | **not L19's** |

**Ruling: L19's "weak-point repair" is the mastery/review mechanism.** It is **not** a mandate to teach, recycle, or rescue French repair language, and L19 must not be read as the home the repair rail has been waiting for. No L19 spec exists, so no document currently makes the wrong claim — this ruling exists to keep it that way.

## 7. Archetype declarations and the prose ↔ schema mapping

The content-archetype axis is declared **independently** of the journey role (archetype templates §12b; Content Bible §6.8). The two must not be inferred from each other.

**A trap this repo has already hit once:** the archetype *prose titles* in `docs/syllabus/lesson-archetype-templates-v1.md` are **not** the `LessonArchetype` enum values in `lemot-app/content/lessonTypes.ts`. L17's first implementation attempt failed typecheck on exactly this (`thematic-vocabulary` → the real value is `thematic-context`).

**The enum has 8 members; the prose index has 11 archetypes. They are not in correspondence.**

| Prose archetype | Schema value |
|---|---|
| 1 · Chunk / Natural Speech / Survival | `chunk-natural-speech` |
| 2 · Architecture Verb | `architecture-verb` |
| 3 · Negation / Question / Social Choice | **no enum member exists** |
| 6 · Pronoun / Particle | `pronoun-particle` |
| 7 · Tense / Aspect / Mood Doorway | `gateway-time-mood` |
| 8 · Pattern Transfer Verb | `pattern-transfer-verb` |
| 9 · Thematic Vocabulary / Context | `thematic-context` |
| 10 · Review / Integration | `review-integration` |
| 11 · Milestone / Transition | `summit-milestone` |
| 4 · Avoir-State, 5 · Object/Article | **no enum member exists** |

### 7.1 L18

**Planning archetype: prose #3, "Negation / Question / Social Choice"** — the pedagogical family a question-word doorway belongs to.

**There is no `negation-question` (or `negation-question-social-choice`) member of `LessonArchetype`.** The planning label appears in the L3, L8, and L13 specs, but no shipped lesson declares it, because it cannot be declared. The two shipped question lessons resolve it the same way:

- `lesson-008.ts` (the `où` question-word doorway) → `primaryArchetype: "chunk-natural-speech"`
- `lesson-012.ts` (the `est-ce que` question doorway) → `primaryArchetype: "chunk-natural-speech"`

**Ruling: L18 follows the L8/L12 precedent — `primaryArchetype: "chunk-natural-speech"`.** The founder direction named `chunk-natural-speech` as L18's *secondary*; since that value must carry the primary slot, **the secondary slot is left open for the compact spec**, with `thematic-context` the candidate (human-context continuity from L17). **Whatever the spec picks must be a real enum member.**

> `[OPEN]` The missing prose-#3 enum member is a **pre-existing schema gap**, not one this decision creates. It is surfaced, not fixed — adding an enum value is a schema change and out of scope here.

### 7.2 L19

**Primary: `review-integration`** (prose #10, Review / Integration) — a real enum member, matching shipped L13 and L16.

**Secondary:** the founder direction says *"appropriate Reading / Human-context."* Prose "Thematic Vocabulary / Context" maps to `thematic-context`; shipped L13 and L16 both chose `chunk-natural-speech` for their comparable flavor slot. **Left to the compact spec**, constrained to a real enum member.

### 7.3 L20

**Primary: `summit-milestone`** (prose #11, Milestone / Transition) — a real enum member. **Secondary: left to the compact spec.**

> Note (archetype templates §12b, `[OPEN]`): `review-integration` and `summit-milestone` are flagged as *legacy mixed-axis values* — they name a journey role rather than a linguistic engine, and await a future metadata reconciliation once a `journeyRole` field is settled. They are **not** deprecated, and lessons declaring them stay valid. L19 and L20 will therefore declare a value on **both** axes that carries the same word. That is expected, not a duplication error.
